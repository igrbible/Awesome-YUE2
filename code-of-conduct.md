# Code of Conduct

This repository is a shared list, and the people who build the projects in it are its readers too.

- Be respectful to contributors and to the authors of every project listed here, including projects that compete with each other.
- Judge projects by what they do. Keep criticism specific, factual and about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, spam or self-promotion outside the contribution rules.
- Do not post private information about anyone.

The maintainers may edit, close or lock any issue or pull request, and may block anyone who repeatedly ignores these rules. To report a problem, open an issue; if it should not be public, open a short issue without the details and ask for a private channel, and the maintainers will arrange one.
